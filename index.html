    Receptors get terrain automatically. The zones are sampled on request and then stay put until the stage moves. Ground heights come from terrain tiles at about 30 m resolution: good for ridges, cliffs and valleys, blind to walls, hedges and buildings.

    4× Funktion‑One Evo 2 on 8× F121: about 110–114 dB(A) at 10 m when driven hard; a comfortable dance floor is 102–106 at the front. Measure once at 10 m with an SPL meter and type it in to calibrate.

    Model: −6 dB per doubling of distance from 10 m, ISO 9613‑1 air absorption for the chosen temperature and humidity, soft‑ground and wind/inversion refraction terms, horn directivity, and single‑edge terrain diffraction. Not modelled: buildings, walls, vegetation, water reflections, multiple ridges. “Worst (ISO)” is a permit convention, not a forecast. A deroga needs a tecnico competente in acustica.
